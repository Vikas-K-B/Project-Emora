Frequently Asked Questions

Is the app free to use?
Yes, Emora is currently in open beta and completely free to download and use.
Does it require an internet connection?
While streaming and initial Gemini AI setup require internet, core playback and local TensorFlow Lite mood inference work completely offline for privacy and convenience.
Does it support Wear OS?
Yes! Emora pulls metrics through Google Health Connect, making it compatible with any Wear OS smartwatch or fitness band that syncs with Health Connect.
Is my health data secure?
Absolutely. Emora uses local on-device processing for your vitals. Your heart rate and step count data is never sold, shared, or sent to external marketing servers.
